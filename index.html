Grammar Reference & Rules

Key Principles

  • Extra Information: A nonessential clause adds extra detail. The sentence remains grammatically complete without it.
  • Comma Rule: Always separate nonessential adjective clauses from the main clause with commas.
  • Relative Pronouns: Begins with who, whom, which, where, when, or whose.
  • The "THAT" Rule: The word THAT is NEVER used in nonessential adjective clauses! Use which instead.

Examples

People: Steve Jobs, who co-founded Apple, was a technology visionary.

Things: ENIAC, which was built in 1945, was a huge computer.

Places: Harvard University, where Bill Gates studied, is in Massachusetts.

Possession: Reshma Saujani, whose dad worked as an engineer, founded Girls Who Code.
